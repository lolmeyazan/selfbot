## Packages
zustand | Global state management for storing Discord tokens in localStorage securely
framer-motion | Smooth cyberpunk animations and glitch effects
lucide-react | Icons (already installed but ensuring available)

## Notes
- `zustand` is used with `persist` middleware to handle the `localStorage` requirement for tokens securely and reactively across the app.
- Discord proxy requests rely on the `/api/discord-proxy` endpoint.
- Cyberpunk theme heavily relies on CSS variables and custom `framer-motion` variants.
